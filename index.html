Each student gets a public fundraising page. The link carries that student's public totals in the address itself — no account, no server. Share it with family, friends, and the church.

How it works

  1. Add your students and set each one's fundraising goal.
  2. Log donations as they come in — cash, check, or online.
  3. Build the budget line by line; per-person items multiply by your headcount automatically.
  4. Share each student's page with their family and supporters.
  5. Print the board report for your next missions-team meeting.
